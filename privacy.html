Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Engineered by RajCode

1. Introduction & Overview

At PixelCraft, accessible from pixelcraft-rajcode.netlify.app, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is not collected, how local storage is handled, and our zero-server data guarantee.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact RajCode via our Contact page.

2. Zero-Server Image Processing (Client-Side Only)

The core architecture of PixelCraft is built around 100% client-side browser processing:

  • No Cloud Uploads: When you select or drag and drop images into PixelCraft, your files are read locally into your browser's RAM via secure Blob URLs. They are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud bucket.
  • Local Rendering: All compression, resizing, conversion, cropping, and photo editing operations are executed locally using the HTML5 Canvas 2D API and WebAssembly / JavaScript libraries.
  • Immediate Memory Purge: Once you close, refresh, or navigate away from the browser tab, all allocated image buffers are instantly discarded by your browser.

3. Log Files, Cookies, and Local Storage

PixelCraft follows a standard procedure of using local web storage. We do not use intrusive third-party tracking cookies, behavioral ad pixels, or cross-site user fingerprinting scripts.

Any preference or state saved during your session exists strictly within your browser's temporary storage to ensure fast tool performance and disappears when your session ends.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

PixelCraft may link to external developer portfolios or open-source repositories. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of PixelCraft immediately.
